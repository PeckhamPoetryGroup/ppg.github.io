Urban Notation

PPG member
> bus brake hiss / loose poster slaps / neon 6:58
> chip-shop salt pinned to fingertip / the mind edits
> a city into couplets / we listen / we write

Cut-Up Flyer (Found Poem)

PPG collage
hand-typed tomorrow / open late // bring your own quiet
lost cat, answers to THUNDER / reward: a soft yes
stairs to the roof — please notice the sky

About

Peckham Poetry Group is a reading and making circle meeting in the library pods at Peckham Library. The group focuses on attentive reading, discussion, and producing new work together.

This site acts as a simple record of sessions and selected works.
