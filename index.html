Urban Notation

by PPG Member
> bus brake hiss / loose poster slaps / neon 6:58
> chip‑shop salt pinned to fingertip / the mind edits
> a city into couplets / we listen / we write

Cut‑Up Flyer (Found Poem)

by PPG Collage
hand‑typed tomorrow / open late // bring your own quiet
lost cat, answers to THUNDER / reward: a soft yes
stairs to the roof — please notice the sky

About

Peckham Poetry Group is a reading & making circle meeting in the library pods. This website is intentionally simple — a public scrapbook of works‑in‑progress, session notes, zine fragments, and experiments. Breaking, changing, and hand‑edited by humans.

Ethos: low‑pressure, high‑attention; early‑internet spirit; collective practice over perfection.
